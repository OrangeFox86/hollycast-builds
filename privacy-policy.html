Privacy Policy for Hollycast

Information collection and use

1. Crash reports

If automatic crash-report uploading is enabled in Hollycast and supported by your build, diagnostic crash reports may be sent to Sentry.io to help the development team investigate problems. Reports can include a minidump and an associated log. For details about Sentry's handling of information, see Sentry's Privacy Policy.

2. Online games

When you use Hollycast to run software that connects to the internet, that software may send and receive data through your connection as it is designed to do. Hollycast does not control what that software communicates or how its servers handle the data.

3. Third-party services

  • When you use the feature to fetch game covers or metadata, Hollycast makes requests to TheGamesDB. Those requests may disclose your network's IP address and information identifying the game you requested. See TheGamesDB for its privacy practices; you can avoid this data sharing by not using the feature.
  • If Discord Presence is enabled and Discord is available on your device, Hollycast can share the game you are playing with Discord to show your activity. See Discord's Privacy Policy. You can stop this sharing by disabling Discord Presence in Hollycast's settings.
